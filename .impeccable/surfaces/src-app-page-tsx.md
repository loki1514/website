# Landing page (/) surface brief

Scope: the partner website home page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Latur shop owners on budget Android phones. Action: start the application (/join). Proof: the order journey itself, shown with real screens of the three apps, and Bible-final rules only. No partner names, prices or commission rates.
Direction set with the user on 14 and 15 Nov 2026. The lasting visual rules are in DESIGN.md; this file keeps what belongs to this page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves the product by playing one order through a pinned phone, in pop-art comic lettering on a calm field of grass. It refuses the dark neon tech hero with a grid of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: A slowly drifting green field seen through faintly fluted glass, with a fine still grass texture and a light dither (moods lime, mint, forest). White comic lettering with an ink outline and one phrase in pop yellow, standing on the field with no box behind it. A slim frosted-glass bar. A titanium phone frame holding real app screens. Yellow starbursts.

STORY: The visitor meets the offer and the first question of the application, then watches an order go customer app → Partner POS → rider app → delivered and in Wednesday's payout, believes it is one simple system run by local people, and starts the application.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The grass field edge to edge. Left: the headline in pop lettering, one line of offer, then "What's your shop called?" with a white pill input and a yellow pill button that carries the answer into /join. Right: the phone, showing the customer app's opening screen. Top: the glass bar with the logo, the three apps as chapters, the language switch, Sign in and Become a partner.

FORM: A pinned-phone scroll story, five stops. Signature interaction: scroll swaps the app screen in the phone, the words beside it and the field's mood, and the header's yellow marker slides to the current app. On a phone the handset sits just under the words. Motion: exponential ease-out, opacity and movement only, calm under reduced motion. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
