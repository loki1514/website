# Landing page (/) surface brief

Scope: the partner website home page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Latur shop owners on budget Android phones. Action: start the application (/join). Proof: one order's path through the three apps, shown with real screens, and Bible-final rules only. No partner names, prices or commission rates.
Direction pinned by the user on 18 Nov 2026 (replaces the pop-art comic world of 14-15 Nov). Lasting visual rules are in DESIGN.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page reads like a survey map of the city with the product laid over it on glass: calm, exact, trustworthy. It refuses both the comic poster it replaced and the dark neon tech hero with a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep green ground carrying slow-moving contour lines and a square dot grid that lights where the land is high. Frosted "Apple glass" panels float on it. Calm cream white for the reading sections; lime only as a signal (primary action, the active stage, the order in motion). No yellow, no outlines-and-hard-shadows, no comic lettering. Type: Anek Latin / Anek Devanagari for headings, Mukta for text. The mascot Ol stays.

STORY: The visitor meets the offer and the first question of the application, sees one order pass customer app, Partner POS, rider app and Wednesday's payout without scrolling, believes it is one simple system run by local people, and starts the application.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Deep green contour field edge to edge. Left: the headline in cream with one phrase in lime, one line of offer, then "What's your shop called?" as a glass field with a lime button that carries the answer into /join. Right: one large glass panel holding the current stage of the order (app name, a sentence, a real app screen rising from its lower edge) under a rail of four stages that advances by itself and can be clicked. Top: a slim dark glass bar with the logo, language switch, Sign in and Become a partner.

FORM: A one-viewport, self-playing order flow (four stages), replacing the five-screen pinned scroll. Signature interaction: the stage rail; changing stage swaps the panel's words and screen and shifts the glow under the contours. Motion: exponential ease-out; contours drift at about 20 frames a second and stop off screen; all still under reduced motion. seed: none (pinned) "contour lines ... with some animation ... with a glass design, apple glass design ... square dot grids ... the yellow should go, the deep green and the lime stays ... calm cream white ... hero section with the concept of the whole app flow"

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
